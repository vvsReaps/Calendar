PH / LESSON 04 CONTINUATION MODEL

DON'T CHASE
THE MOVE.
READ THE INVITE.

A continuation is not “price is moving, so enter.” It is a higher-timeframe draw, a completed internal objective, and a failed reaction that tells you delivery is not finished.

START THE LESSON ↓
THE CORE PRINCIPLE / 01
“There has to be something left for price to go take.”

DRAW FIRST. SETUP SECOND. EXECUTION LAST.

HTF DRAW→OBJECTIVE MET→REACTION→DISRESPECT→CONTINUATION

THE FRAMEWORK

LOGIC BEFORE
LOW TIME FRAME.

Every continuation begins with one question: where is price being drawn, and why is that draw still valid? A clean one-minute setup cannot create a higher-timeframe draw that does not exist.

CONTINUATION HAS A REASON.

Continuation does not mean entering anywhere in the direction price is flowing. It means the higher-timeframe and lower-timeframe stories agree, and there is still a logical objective for price to reach.

NO DRAW, NO TRADE.

If price has already taken the available highs or lows, stop looking for continuation in that direction. Displacement, SMT, or a clean reversal sequence cannot replace a missing draw.

INTERNAL VS. EXTERNAL.

An internal high, low, SIBI, or displacement candle may create a reaction on the way to the main external draw. Treat that reaction as a possible rebalance or manipulation, not an automatic reversal.

LET THE OBJECTIVE REACT.

Never enter simply because price touched the objective. Wait for its reaction, then wait for the reaction to be disrespected. The same execution may still be available minutes later with far better confirmation.

THE VALID CONTINUATION

THE DRAW IS STILL OPEN.

HTF narrative and intraday delivery agree. Price has taken an internal objective, but the main external objective remains untouched.

  • Clear directional draw
  • Speed through internals
  • Room to the main objective

THE FORCED CONTINUATION

NOTHING LEFT TO TAKE.

Once the objective is complete, momentum alone is not trade logic. Do not manufacture a target because displacement looks good.

  • Nearby draws already taken
  • Entering from FOMO
  • Target chosen after entry

AFTER AN OBJECTIVE IS TAKEN

WAIT FOR PRICE
TO SHOW ITS HAND.

The first touch of an internal high, low, displacement candle, or liquidity pool is not the entry. Let the objective produce a reaction. Then wait for price to disrespect that reaction.

01

OBJECTIVE MET

Price reaches an internal draw on the way to the external target.

02

REACTION

Let the pullback or manipulation invite the other side.

03

DISRESPECT

The reversal cannot sustain and price resumes with intent.

04

EXECUTION

Define invalidation beyond the rebalance and target the open draw.

EXECUTION NOTES FROM THE LESSON

WHAT YOU ARE
ACTUALLY WAITING FOR.

At every objective—an internal high or low, a SIBI, a displacement candle, or external liquidity—price is allowed to react. Your edge is not predicting that first reaction. Your edge is recognizing when it cannot hold.

MANIPULATION.

Price may sweep a nearby low on a bullish day or a nearby high on a bearish day, creating the appearance of reversal. If the main draw remains open, this can be the invitation that creates your continuation entry.

REBALANCE, THEN DISRESPECT.

Let price revisit the displacement leg. When it rebalances and then refuses to trade back through it, the rejection shows that delivery toward the main draw is resuming.

SOUP SEQUENCE FAILURE.

Three candles can appear to deliver away from an objective. Do not blindly enter the fourth. Wait for a rebalance and the disrespect of that rebalance; that is what turns context into an execution.

VOLATILITY CHANGES THE SHAPE.

Near the open, price may manipulate and deliver almost immediately. The sequence can be fast, but the rule does not change: identify the draw, read the reaction, and let candle closure confirm the failure.

TURTLE MODEL FAILED

THE REVERSAL
CAN'T CLOSE.

On a bullish day with a higher-timeframe draw above, a bearish Turtle Model is expected to fail. If the confirming bearish candle never closes, sellers have been invited—but not rewarded.

THE READ

The failure to close bearish is information. Expect the SMT and failed reversal structure to be taken as price continues higher.

  • 01 Bullish HTF narrative
  • 02 External draw above
  • 03 Bearish close fails
  • 04 Reclaim confirms

SOUP SEQUENCE FAILED

THE PULLBACK
IS THE INVITE.

This often forms at an internal draw while price is delivering toward the main external draw. In strong bullish delivery, a sudden pullback can be manipulation—not a new bearish narrative.

US VS. THE MARKET

The market invites sellers, uses their stops as fuel, and continues toward the objective. Wait for the invite to fail, then trade the move that stops them.

LIVE CASE STUDY / BEARISH CONTINUATION

THE MOVE THAT
TRAPS THE CROWD.

HTF narrative was bearish. The open manipulated higher, rebalanced a four-hour SIBI, then began delivering toward the external draw below. NQ and ES were moving down with speed and efficiency when price suddenly flipped higher.

01 / CONTEXT

NO MAJOR DRAW WAS TAKEN.

Neither NQ nor ES reached a meaningful objective before the bullish flip. With the bearish external draw still open, the move higher had the profile of manipulation—not a new narrative.

02 / THE INVITE

BUYERS SEE A REVERSAL.

The sudden rally invites breakout buyers and keeps traders married to longs from the opening manipulation. Their participation builds liquidity for continuation lower.

03 / CONFIRMATION

THE CANDLE FAILS AT THE 9 EMA.

In real time, the candle looked like bullish displacement. At closure, it failed to hold above the 9 EMA. That failure preserved the bearish continuation idea.

THE DISCIPLINE

Wait for candle closure. Intrabar movement creates urgency; the close reveals whether price actually held.
