One NQ is too much size for a $50K account

The average stop on this model is $1,094 — 55% of the entire $2,000 allowance on a single trade. The widest is $1,980, or 99% of it. 52% of all setups risk more than half the drawdown at once.

Every account that died here died to one or two ordinary losing trades, not a bad run. On a $150K programme, or on micros, the same signals sit inside the limits comfortably. Read the 6 blow-ups as a sizing verdict, not a signal-quality one.

Payout caps, not performance, set the pace

The unconstrained 1-contract account made $78,013 over the same year. Only 21% of that reached the trader, because each request is capped and the five-winning-day counter resets after every one. $45,303 was still stranded in the account at the end.

Modelled with no partial at the first target, since 30% of one contract is not executable — which is why the win rate in this section runs lower than elsewhere on the page. Payout cap taken as $2,000 per request; Topstep's help centre quotes $2,000 for a $50K Express Funded Account, or $4,000 with the optional daily-loss-limit feature, under which the same run nets $31,770. Eval price of $90 is an assumption — Topstep currently lists $49/month plus a $149 activation on passing, so the real fee drag is higher and recurring. Read this before you read anything above.
  • Hypothetical backtested results — not a live track record.
  • Everything above is gross. Charged at half a point of slippage per leg plus $4 round-turn commission, the year finishes at $50,440, a +100.9% return — costs take roughly a seventh of the result.
  • Fills are modelled at the close of the signal candle and at the exact stop or target price. Real fills gap, slip and queue.
  • Past performance does not predict future results. A single year is one market regime — the final four months of this sample were considerably better than the first eight, for every configuration tested.
  • The first target banks 30% of position. On a single NQ contract that is not executable; it needs micros (3 of 10 MNQ) or a two-contract minimum.
  • Futures trading carries substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. You can lose more than your initial deposit. Nothing here is financial advice, a solicitation, or a recommendation.
  • Results come from bar-close reproductions of the indicator, not TradingView's strategy tester. Absolute setup counts may differ from other runs.
