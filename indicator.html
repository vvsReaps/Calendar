Trailing the exit is what makes a funded account survivable

The shipped record runs every trade to the far target and earns the most on paper, $64,379 against $55,846 trailed. But it produces 4 losing months, a $13,356 drawdown, 42 trades losing over $1,000 and a worst day of −$3,751. Against a $2,000 loss limit that is fatal, repeatedly.

The trail option, armed from entry, holds the year to 1 losing month, a $3,909 drawdown and a worst day of −$1,842, and carries the Lucid run: 2 evaluations, 1 blow-up, $42,182 net. The four losing months of the shipped record (−17.3R combined) flip to +9.7R under the trail. Tick it for any funded account.

Do not strip the account back to the buffer

Lucid lets you withdraw everything above $52,100, which leaves you on a $2,000 leash the next morning. This run withdraws only down to $54,000, keeping $1,900 of extra cushion above the buffer, and returns $42,182. The withdrawal schedule is worth more than the strategy.

Funded accounts use INTRADAY drawdown

Modelled at trade close. Funded accounts charge drawdown at its intrabar worst: a trade that probes deep before resolving can breach the limit even when it closes green. The trail is the margin here: it holds the worst day to −$1,842, where the untrailed exits allow −$3,751 against a $2,000 limit.

Before you read the numbers

What this is, and what it is not

Read this before you read anything above.
  • Hypothetical backtested results, not a live track record.
  • Everything above is gross. Charged at half a point of slippage per leg plus $4 round-turn commission ($24 a trade, $6,168 across 257 trades), the year finishes at +$58,211, a +116.4% return; costs take roughly a tenth of the result.
  • Fills are modelled at the close of the signal candle and at the exact stop or target price. Real fills gap, slip and queue.
  • Past performance does not predict future results. A single year is one market regime: the final four months of this sample were considerably better than the first eight, for every configuration tested.
  • The first target banks 30% of position. On a single NQ contract that is not executable; it needs micros (3 of 10 MNQ) or a two-contract minimum.
  • Futures trading carries substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. You can lose more than your initial deposit. Nothing here is financial advice, a solicitation, or a recommendation.
  • Results come from an independently rebuilt bar-close replay engine (the third build of this model outside TradingView), not TradingView’s strategy tester; it reproduces the reference entry (2026-08-06 09:36 → target). Absolute setup counts may differ from other runs.
