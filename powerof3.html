Power of 3

Price builds liquidity, creates a false move, then delivers toward its objective. The framework is known as AMD.

AMD
The formula

Three phases. One market story.

A

Accumulation

Orders and liquidity build while price trades sideways.

→
M

Manipulation

Price moves against the expected direction and invites the wrong position.

→
D

Delivery

Price expands toward the real draw with directional intent.

01 / The phases

Understand what price is doing now.

01

Accumulation

Buyers and sellers accept a relatively fair area. Price moves horizontally, repeatedly trading up and down without producing meaningful directional progress.

  • Liquidity forms above highs and below lows
  • Directional structure remains unclear
  • Price prints through time rather than distance
  • This is preparation, not delivery
02

Manipulation

Price leaves the range in the direction opposite the anticipated delivery. That false move encourages traders to commit to the wrong side and places their stops into usable liquidity.

  • Often raids a nearby high or low
  • Creates fear, urgency, and FOMO
  • Must be interpreted through narrative
  • Do not assume every breakout is delivery
03

Delivery

After liquidity has been engineered and collected, price expands toward the actual draw. This phase may also be called expansion or continuation.

  • Directional movement becomes productive
  • Speed and displacement should increase
  • Price seeks the narrative-aligned draw
  • This is the phase traders want to capture
Trade the delivery, not the setup work.Accumulation creates the liquidity. Manipulation clears the wrong side. Delivery is where the narrative becomes productive.
02 / Psychology

Why the false move works.

Manipulation makes the wrong direction feel obvious immediately before price delivers elsewhere.

01

Both sides build positions

During accumulation, longs and shorts enter around the perceived fair price while stops collect outside the range.

02

The market presents a false answer

The manipulation break convinces traders that direction has finally been revealed.

03

Liquidity is collected

When price turns, wrong-side traders exit or are stopped, helping fuel the real move.

04

Price delivers to the draw

The expansion expresses the broader narrative once the necessary liquidity has been engineered.

04 / Narrative

AMD is a framework, not a pattern.

The narrative tells you which side should be manipulation and where delivery should seek.

01

What is the higher-timeframe bias and active dealing range?

02

What liquidity draw gives price a logical objective?

03

Is price accumulating, manipulating, or already delivering?

04

Did the false move collect liquidity opposite the expected delivery?

05

Has candle closure and real displacement confirmed the direction?

Do not label every consolidation and breakout as Power of 3. Without narrative and a draw, it is only a shape.
05 / Fractal time

The model repeats across every timeframe.

Time is fractal

AMD inside AMD.

A larger accumulation can contain several smaller Power of 3 cycles before the higher-timeframe delivery begins.

A quick one-minute model and a multi-hour model may look different only because of candle count. Compress the longer structure onto a higher timeframe and the same three-phase story becomes visible.

Do not pattern trade a fixed number of candles. Identify the purpose of each phase and the liquidity relationship between them.

What to observe

Sideways price builds fuel.

The examples begin with price bouncing inside a range, generating liquidity without making clear highs or lows.

What changes

The false move sets the trap.

Manipulation moves opposite the eventual draw, attracting longs in a bearish model or shorts in a bullish model.

What matters

Delivery completes the story.

Once the trap is cleared, price expands toward the objective. Multiple cycles may appear inside one another across timeframes.

Applying Power of 3 to continuations

Use narrative, SMT, location, rebalancing, and displacement to participate in an existing delivery.

Read the environment

Know the type of delivery.

A continuation may stair-step through higher highs and higher lows, or expand directly before rebalancing. Identify the market you are in instead of forcing one shape.

Wait for confirmation

Rebalance, then displacement.

Let price rebalance into a logical area. Wait for accumulation and manipulation, then require a displacement candle out of the range before considering entry.

Respect location

Do not chase the last trade.

Avoid shorts too deep in discount and longs too high in premium. The continuation must still have a clear draw on liquidity with room to deliver.
